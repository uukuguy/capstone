# PyPSA Capacity Planning

Use only published `planning.capacity_expand`, `planning.capacity_commitment`, `planning.multi_period`, `planning.stochastic`, and `planning.near_optimal_capacity` with an application-issued current-run model handoff. Describe capacity, commitment, pathway years, scenario recourse, and costs only from target-owned result and evidence references. For MGA, the objective is installed capacity; report system cost separately and do not describe the alternative objective as cost. Do not claim unregistered investment data or sector-coupled planning.
